# Gharelu — Ghar Jaisa Khana

A warm AI kitchen companion built on two mothers' real wisdom — for hostellers, newly married couples, working women, and anyone living away from home.

---

## What it does

Tell Gharelu what's in your kitchen. It replies in Hinglish — warm, specific, practical — the way a mother would. Three modes:

- **Cook** — one good dish from what you have right now
- **Store** — how to keep ingredients fresh the way maa does  
- **Nourish** — how to eat what you made, what to pair, when

## Who it's for

Hostellers, newly married couples, working women, men who grew up watching someone cook for them — anyone who finds themselves alone in front of a fridge at 9 PM.
