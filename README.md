# Gharelu — Ghar Jaisa Khana

A warm AI kitchen companion built on two mothers' real wisdom — for hostellers, newly married couples, working women, and anyone living away from home.

---

## What it does

Tell Gharelu what's in your kitchen. It replies in Hinglish — warm, specific, practical — the way a mother would. Three modes:

- **Cook** — one good dish from what you have right now
- **Store** — how to keep ingredients fresh the way maa does  
- **Nourish** — how to eat what you made: what to pair, when, and what helps when you're feeling heavy, acidic, low on energy, fighting a cold, on your period, or can't sleep

## Who it's for

Hostellers, newly married couples, working women, men who grew up watching someone cook for them — anyone who finds themselves alone in front of a fridge at 9 PM.

## Out of scope (for now)

What Gharelu deliberately does **not** do — so the scope stays honest:

- **No calorie counting, macros, or diet/weight-loss tracking** — this is a core stance, not a missing feature.
- **No accounts, saved recipes, or meal history** — every conversation starts fresh.
- **No grocery lists or weekly meal planning.**
- **Store is intentionally basic** — it works, but hasn't been built out beyond core freshness advice. Cook and Nourish are where the depth lives today.
